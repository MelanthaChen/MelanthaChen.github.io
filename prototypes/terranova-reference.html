Finding

The glass itself is convincing, but the near-white background carries pale highlights that are too close to the subject for reliable color-key removal. A production-ready transparent video would require offline roto/segmentation with manual edge cleanup, or a source render with a proper alpha channel. The portfolio has not been changed.
